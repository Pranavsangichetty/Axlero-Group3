# Customer Retention Strategy

## Objective

Define segment-specific actions using the existing RFM segmentation.

| Segment | Business Objective | Recommended Actions | Suggested KPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Champions | Retain high-value customers and encourage advocacy. | Offer VIP rewards, early product access, referral benefits and exclusive loyalty perks. | Repeat purchase rate, referral participation, revenue per customer |
| Loyal High-Value | Reactivate valuable customers. | Send personalized win-back emails, loyalty reminders and relevant product recommendations. | Reactivation rate, repeat purchase rate, recovered revenue |
| Recent Customers | Convert recent buyers into repeat customers. | Use welcome campaigns, second-purchase incentives and product recommendations. | Second-purchase rate, time to second purchase |
| At-Risk / Hibernating | Reactivate customers where commercially worthwhile. | Test targeted re-engagement campaigns, feedback surveys and carefully controlled discounts. | Reactivation rate, campaign conversion, campaign ROI |

## Campaign Measurement

- Establish a baseline before each campaign.
- Compare campaign outcomes with an appropriate control group where possible.
- Track incremental revenue, conversion and campaign costs.
- Avoid excessive discounts where they reduce profitability.
- Review segment movement regularly using consistently defined RFM snapshots.

## Limitations

These recommendations are proposed strategies, not experimentally proven results. Test campaigns and measure their actual performance before scaling them.
